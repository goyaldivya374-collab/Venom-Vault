FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
How do I earn VaultCoins? ▼

You earn VaultCoins by winning games, logging in daily, leaving reviews, completing achievements, and referring friends. Every action inside VenomVault rewards you with coins that never expire.

Is VenomVault free to use? ▼

Yes! VenomVault is completely free to use. Create an account, play games, and earn coins at no cost. We offer optional premium features for players who want extra perks, but all core games are free.

How does the anti-cheat system work? ▼

Our real-time monitoring system flags unusual patterns like impossible scores, speed hacks, and bot activity. Flagged accounts are reviewed by our team within 24 hours. Cheaters are permanently banned.

Can I suggest a new game? ▼

Absolutely! Use the contact form above with subject "Game Suggestion" and describe the type of game you'd like to see. Popular suggestions are reviewed monthly and the best ones get added to the Vault!

My VaultCoins are missing — what do I do? ▼

Send us a message via the contact form with your account details and we'll investigate within 2 hours. Coin issues are our top priority and are almost always resolved within the same day.
