OUR MISSION
VenomVault exists to break the barriers between players. We believe every gamer — regardless of skill, background, or device — deserves a world-class gaming experience. Our platform delivers personalised games, fair competition, and real rewards to players across the globe. Gaming is not just entertainment. It's a culture, a community, and a craft. We're here to honour that.

THE VAULT TIMELINE

JANUARY 2023
🌱 THE IDEA IS BORN
Developer Arjun Sharma sketches the first concept for VenomVault — a personalised gaming hub with a built-in rewards economy. Started as a college project with big ambitions.
JUNE 2023
🛠️ FIRST BUILD
The first prototype goes live with 3 games and a basic login system. Early testers give overwhelmingly positive feedback on the personalisation quiz and VaultCoin concept.
NOVEMBER 2023
🚀 BETA LAUNCH
VenomVault enters public beta with 12 games, tournament support, and the full VaultCoin economy. 10,000 players join within the first month. Anti-cheat system deployed.
MARCH 2024
⭐ V1.0 OFFICIAL RELEASE
Full public launch. 48 games available across 6 genres. Live leaderboards, global tournaments, and the Vault Rank system go live. Player count hits 500,000.
JANUARY 2025
🏆 1 MILLION PLAYERS
VenomVault crosses 1 million registered players across 50+ countries. Shadow Protocol becomes the platform's first mega-hit title with 200,000+ daily active users.
MAY 2025 — NOW
🌍 GLOBAL EXPANSION
VenomVault launches dedicated servers in 12 regions worldwide, introduces mobile support, and begins partnerships with indie game developers. Season 3 of Shadow Protocol breaks all records.
